What an affiliate relationship means

An affiliate relationship allows a retailer or service provider to attribute a purchase to a link on this site. The provider may then pay us a commission. The amount and eligibility rules are set by the provider, and a visitor does not pay us to use the calculators.

Affiliate revenue helps support hosting, maintenance, research, and continued access to the site's free tools. It does not change the formulas used by the calculators or the scenarios they produce.

How affiliate links are identified

Pages that contain affiliate links include a nearby disclosure and identify the relationship in the link wording, such as "paid link." Paid links also use the standard rel="sponsored" attribute so browsers and search engines can identify their commercial nature.

We aim to describe products and services accurately and to distinguish scenarios from verified facts. A commission does not guarantee positive coverage, placement, or a recommendation. Calculator results use visible user-supplied inputs and, where applicable, plainly cited fixed rules; affiliate compensation does not change the formula.
