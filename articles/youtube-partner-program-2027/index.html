YouTube Partner Program Changes for February 2027

Review the announced terms deadline, identify which eligibility test applies, and keep post-share arithmetic separate from qualification.

Which change affects your channel?

Existing partners should review the updated modules in YouTube Studio by January 31, 2027. New applicants face higher ads and Premium entry thresholds from February 1. Being in YPP and qualifying for monthly Shorts earnings are separate tests.

Start with the Earn tab in your own Studio account. Record your channel status and qualified metrics there; public view counts alone cannot establish eligibility. Meeting a numerical threshold does not guarantee approval or any payout.

Shorts: an existing partner versus a new applicant

Synthetic eligibility example: An existing YPP channel with 8M qualified Shorts views over 90 days is below the new 10M earning floor. At 12M, it clears that floor, assuming the other conditions are met. Neither number proves a new applicant meets the 20M Shorts entry route. The comparison describes eligibility, not dollars earned.

YouTube says Shorts revenue sharing resumes automatically once the threshold is crossed again. Other earning features are not a promise of replacement income; use confirmed dashboard figures for budgeting.

Keep shares, pools and your own revenue rate separate

CPM measures advertiser spending per ad impression before revenue share. RPM uses creator revenue after sharing and can include Premium, memberships and Supers. That broader RPM is not an ad-only rate. Match the numerator, view denominator, format and reporting period.

Synthetic share comparison: A hypothetical $100 eligible long-form net-ad base at 55% leaves $55. A hypothetical $100 Shorts Creator Pool allocation at 45% leaves $45. These are different bases; this does not mean the same views earn either amount.

Synthetic calculator example: If your chosen ad-only, post-share assumption is $2 per 1,000 total views, 50,000 total views produce $100: 50,000 ÷ 1,000 × $2. Do not multiply that $100 by 55% again. The $2 is invented for arithmetic, not a benchmark.

A practical review checklist

  1. Review each updated module offered in Studio and retain your acceptance record.
  2. Record whether you are already a partner or applying, and which earning feature you are checking.
  3. Use qualified metrics from the Earn tab, with the correct measurement window.
  4. Keep long-form, Shorts, subscription and other income records separate before combining net amounts.
  5. Recheck the linked official pages when your account status changes or before relying on a future rule.

The calculator cannot establish eligibility, accept terms for you, predict rates, or reproduce the platform’s pool allocation. Your worksheet stays on your device; your spreadsheet app controls its own storage and syncing.
