What “evidence-first” means here

  • A guide answers a real decision that an existing calculator cannot answer on its own.
  • Changeable platform facts link to current primary documentation and carry a review date.
  • Examples are synthetic and labeled; they are never presented as typical creator results.
  • Every calculation exposes its inputs and limits, and every recommendation remains optional.
  • Article structured data describes only content that is visible on the page.

The library will grow deliberately. Publishing fewer useful guides is safer and more helpful than recreating every keyword variation as a separate page.
