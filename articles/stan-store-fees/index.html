How Much Are Stan Store Fees?

Stan Store fees in the calculator’s U.S. monthly-plan comparison start with a plan cost of $29 to $99 per month, depending on the plan. Card processing adds a per-sale charge that varies with product price and sales volume. Across the example below, the modeled Stan plan and card fees total $43.60 to $134 per month. Payout charges are separate, so your bank deposit can differ from the calculator’s net estimate.

What fees does the Stan Store calculator include?

The calculator uses a monthly plan cost and a per-sale processing cost. In its U.S. domestic-card scenario, the sales charge combines a percentage of the product price with a fixed charge per sale. The tool shows Stan payout fees separately because your payout schedule affects how often those fees apply. The fee assumptions come from the calculator’s October 24, 2026 snapshot.

Net here means monthly gross sales minus the listed plan and sale fees. To reproduce the calculation, multiply price by monthly sales. Then multiply the per-sale fee by the same sales count and subtract that total and the plan fee from gross sales. Taxes, refunds, disputes, international-card costs and currency conversion are outside the model.

How do I calculate what I’ll keep on Stan?

Start with the same hypothetical $40 to $50 selling price and 10 to 20 monthly sales. Gross sales span $400 to $1,000. On Creator, the modeled card fees span $14.60 to $35.00, depending on price and sales volume. Subtract those card fees and the Creator plan cost from the matching gross endpoints to get a net range of $356.40 to $936.00 before payout fees and other costs.

Which Stan Store plan should I choose?

This comparison is best for a creator with one main product price and a realistic range of monthly sales. It is a poor fit for forecasting payout deposits, modeling mixed prices or comparing stores whose sales volumes would differ because of their features or reach.

Compare both plans at your slow and busy sales levels. The model uses the same per-sale card fee on Creator and Creator Pro, so the higher plan cost needs to be justified by features you expect to use. Check the plan features and your account’s fee terms before choosing.

Does covering the Stan plan mean I broke even?

The calculator’s cover-plan sales figure tells you how many sales it takes for revenue after card fees to cover the monthly plan. It divides the plan cost by the amount you keep per sale after processing, then rounds up to a whole sale. To work out whether the business breaks even, include other costs such as refunds, fulfillment and payouts.

What else do creators ask about Stan Store fees?

Does the estimated net match my bank deposit?

The calculator subtracts monthly plan and sale fees from gross sales. Your deposit can differ because the model shows payout fees separately and leaves out adjustments such as refunds and currency conversion.

Will selling more make Creator Pro cheaper than Creator?

The modeled card charge is the same on both plans, so more sales alone leave the monthly plan cost gap unchanged. Judge the upgrade by whether the additional features are worth that gap to you.

Do the Gumroad numbers include marketplace sales?

The table uses the calculator’s direct-sale scenario. Sales through Gumroad’s Discover marketplace use a separate fee structure, so estimate them separately if you rely on that channel.
