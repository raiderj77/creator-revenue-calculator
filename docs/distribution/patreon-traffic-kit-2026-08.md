## Verified product claims

- Lets the creator select a current or eligible legacy Patreon plan; the tool does not verify legacy-plan eligibility.
- Models the listed USD payment-method profiles.
- Accepts up to four user-entered tier prices and patron counts.
- Accepts an optional user-entered observed churn rate and monthly take-home target.
- Shows gross revenue, modeled platform and processing fees, monthly and annualized net, and a tier breakdown.
- Solves a target from the entered tier mix without supplying growth, conversion, or churn benchmarks.
- Supports copy, print/PDF, and sharing of the canonical tool URL without result values.
- Provides a blank CSV for reconciling actual Patreon payouts separately from modeled scenarios.
- Requires no account and does not upload calculator or tracker values.

Official references:

- <https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/11111747095181-Creator-fees-overview>
- <https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/15458893135501-How-payments-reach-you>

## Claims not to make

- Do not claim typical, average, guaranteed, or likely creator earnings.
- Do not call a modeled result an actual payout or forecast.
- Do not imply Patreon affiliation, endorsement, or certification.
- Do not claim every possible fee is included.
- Do not claim tracker downloads or tool-link shares are measured; those outcomes are currently unknown.
- Do not add follower, niche, conversion, growth, churn, or audience benchmarks.

## Launch copy

### Product name

Creator Revenue Calculator — Patreon Fee & Income Tracker

### Tagline

Model Patreon fees. Track actual payouts separately.

### Short description

Build a Patreon fee scenario from your plan, USD payment method, tier prices, and patron counts. Review modeled fees and take-home, solve a user-entered target, and download a blank CSV for actual payout records.

### Long description

Creator Revenue Calculator keeps fee scenarios separate from actual Patreon payouts. Enter your current plan, USD payment method, and up to four membership tiers. The tool shows gross revenue, modeled platform and processing fees, monthly and annualized net, churn impact from your own entered rate, and the gap to your own take-home target.

Every amount starts at zero. The calculator supplies no typical earnings, audience-growth rate, conversion rate, or churn benchmark. A blank CSV is included for recording actual fees, adjustments, patron changes, and payouts from Patreon records.

Free to use. No Creator Revenue Calculator account required. Independent tool; not affiliated with Patreon.

### Maker comment

Creator-income calculators often hide assumptions or present a scenario as an earnings prediction. We built this tool around the opposite approach: every input is visible, every amount starts at zero, and the result states what is included and excluded.

The target solver uses only the creator's entered tier mix and fee profile. The downloadable CSV is intentionally separate so hypothetical scenarios are not confused with actual payout records.

Feedback we would value: Is the distinction between planning and payout reconciliation clear? Is the target solver useful without supplying a growth benchmark?
