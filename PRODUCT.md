# PRODUCT.md: Creator Revenue Calculator

Written 2026-11-07 from the live site and the repo, so design and build work starts from what the product actually is.

## What it is

creatorrevenuecalculator.com: free revenue calculators for online creators (YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, Instagram, podcast, sponsorship, affiliate, UGC, engagement, newsletter, Patreon, Stan Store, and a combined social earnings estimator), plus articles that show the math behind the numbers.

## Who it serves

Creators estimating what their audience is worth before they set rates, sign deals, or pick a platform. The honest edge: every calculator shows its assumptions, and articles reproduce their worked figures from stated inputs (the repo's test suites enforce that).

## Money path

- Email capture to the Beehiiv list (the Creator publication), live on the homepage and all 11 tool pages since Nov 2026.
- Affiliate recommendations where they fit the tool, under the site's affiliate disclosure.
- PDFs and packs sell through the Etsy and Payhip shops (DevelopVault). The site itself does not sell PDFs on-page; it points buyers to the shops.

## Register and voice

Plain, direct, numbers first. The audience is working creators, not marketers. No hype claims, no invented proof, no earnings promises. Where a platform's own numbers differ from the calculator's assumptions, say so on the page.

## Design direction (summary, details in DESIGN.md)

Purple-led, Poppins headings with Inter body, light and dark modes both supported. The product reads as a tool first: calculator above the fold, answer capsules for the short version, articles for the deep version.

## Hard constraints

- Calculators stay free, forever. Paid products are add-ons (packs, exports), never a gate on a calculator.
- No fabricated numbers, testimonials, or proof anywhere.
- Merge and deploy approvals are the owner's. Changes land on branches first.
