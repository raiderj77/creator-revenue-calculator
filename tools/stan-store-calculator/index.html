Fees and limits used

Stan: Creator is $29/month ($300/year); Creator Pro is $99/month ($948/year). Stan states a 0% platform transaction fee. This scenario uses US Stripe standard purchases at 2.9% + $0.30 per sale. Stan also lists a payout fee of 0.25% + $0.25 per payout, $1 minimum; it is displayed separately and is not included in the modeled net because payout frequency is not entered.

Gumroad: this comparison uses its 10% + $0.50 direct/profile-sale rate, not the 30% Discover rate. Direct Stripe: this uses Stripe's standard US domestic-card rate of 2.9% + $0.30, with no monthly Stripe plan fee included.

Annual Stan subscriptions can lower the effective monthly subscription amount to $25 for Creator or $79 for Creator Pro, but this comparison uses monthly plans. Visit Stan directly.

Sources reviewed October 24, 2026: Stan transaction fees, Stan plan comparison, Gumroad pricing, and Stripe pricing. Taxes, refunds, disputes, international-card and currency-conversion charges, payment methods other than standard domestic cards, and account-specific costs are outside this scenario.
