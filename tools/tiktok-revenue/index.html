How the TikTok revenue scenario works

Creator Rewards revenue equals qualified views divided by 1,000 and multiplied by the observed reward value you enter. Brand revenue equals completed deals multiplied by the net retained fee. LIVE and other confirmed revenue are added exactly as entered.

Why this tool does not use the former Creator Fund

Program availability, eligibility, qualified-view rules, and rewards can change by region and account. The current program information and the user's in-app dashboard are better sources than a historical public Creator Fund range. This calculator therefore starts every variable rate at zero.

What the calculator does not include

It does not decide whether views qualify, estimate a brand's willingness to pay, infer income from followers or engagement, convert gifts or Diamonds, model refunds, or calculate taxes. Reconcile each result with dashboard, invoice, contract, and retained-payment records.
