How the Twitch revenue scenario works

The worksheet keeps each revenue stream separate so an uncertain assumption cannot silently inflate another line. Subscription revenue is subscription count multiplied by the net amount you enter. Ad revenue is monetized impressions divided by 1,000 and multiplied by your entered post-share value. Completed sponsorship revenue is added exactly as entered.

Why there is no default subscription or ad rate

Local subscription pricing, the creator's agreement, Plus Program qualification, refunds, taxes, geography, ad fill, and reporting adjustments can change the net result. A public universal rate would hide those differences. Replace zero defaults with figures from the same Twitch reporting period.

Bits source and limitation

Twitch Creator Camp states that eligible Partners and Affiliates receive $0.01 for every Bit used to Cheer directly in their channel. Viewer purchase cost and Bits used in extensions can be treated differently, so this line is limited to the channel-chat use case.

What the calculator does not include

It does not model taxes, refunds, Prime subscription differences, local pricing, donations outside Twitch, merchandise, agency fees, payout fees, unbooked sponsors, or contract-specific adjustments. Reconcile the result with retained payout and invoice records before making a business decision.

Subscription splits and Plus Points

The Twitch Affiliate FAQ describes the standard Affiliate subscription split as 50/50 after applicable deductions. Your agreement and dashboard determine your actual net amount; do not multiply a public subscription sticker price by the split and assume that is your payout.

The Plus Program generally requires 100 Plus Points for Level 1 (60/40) or 300 for Level 2 (70/30), maintained for three consecutive months. Qualification lasts 12 months from the most recent qualification date. Latin America has lower thresholds: 50 and 200 points. Check your dashboard and monetized streamer agreement.

Recurring paid Tier 1, Tier 2 and Tier 3 subscriptions earn 1, 2 and 6 points respectively. Gift subscriptions earn no points but receive the enhanced split once qualified. Prime subscriptions neither earn points nor receive that enhanced split.

Enter the net per-subscription amount from your reports. Keep Prime payouts separate when they differ. The calculator does not apply a second revenue share to your net input.
