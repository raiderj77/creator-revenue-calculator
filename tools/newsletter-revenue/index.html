How the newsletter scenario works

Every result is traceable to a visible field.

Subscription formula

Paid subscribers are either entered directly or calculated from list size multiplied by your conversion assumption. Gross subscription revenue equals paid subscribers multiplied by monthly price. The platform fee is the gross amount multiplied by the percentage you enter.

Completed-work rule

Sponsorship and referral lines use completed or confirmed counts multiplied by the net amount entered. This avoids turning an unbooked offer, public CPM claim, or unapproved referral into revenue.

Costs and annualization

Other monthly costs are subtracted once. The annual figure simply multiplies the monthly scenario by twelve; it does not model churn, growth, seasonality, taxes, currency conversion, refunds not entered, or changing platform terms.

Records to retain

  • The reporting period and paid-subscriber count.
  • The billing record or agreement supporting the platform-fee input.
  • Completed sponsorship invoices and confirmed referral reports.
  • Processing, software, refund, contractor, and operating costs included in the scenario.
