How does the social media earnings estimator work?

Choose a platform or revenue stream and a scenario type. For platform-payout and sponsorship-value scenarios, the tool divides your entered quantity by 1,000 and multiplies it by your entered rate. For user-entered revenue, it uses the monthly amount you provide.

Platform payout scenario

Enter a comparable monthly quantity and your own revenue per 1,000 units. The tool does not supply or infer a platform rate.

Sponsorship-value scenario

Enter the sponsored quantity and the rate you deliberately want to quote or test. This is not a market benchmark.

User-entered revenue

Enter an attributable monthly amount from your records or a deliberate plan. Keep unlike revenue definitions in separate rows.
