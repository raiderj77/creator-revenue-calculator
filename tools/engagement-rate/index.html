Interpretation

A rate is only comparable when the platform, post type, date range, selected engagement actions, and denominator match.

Sponsorship use

Engagement can inform a media kit, but this tool does not convert a rate into a sponsorship price.

What the result means

The calculator reports arithmetic, not a percentile, quality grade, or platform benchmark.

Platforms define and expose engagement metrics differently. Record the exact formula alongside any rate you publish.

How to calculate engagement rate consistently

Choose one numerator and one denominator before comparing posts or accounts. This tool's numerator is likes plus comments plus shares plus saves. The follower-based denominator is followers or subscribers. The view-based denominator is views or impressions.

Why there is no universal benchmark

A rate changes when the platform exposes different actions, when a post receives paid distribution, when the denominator is followers instead of reach, or when a different content format is selected. A universal low, average, or excellent tier would conceal those differences.

How to use the result

Save the platform, content type, date range, sample size, numerator fields, and denominator with the result. For trend analysis, repeat the exact same method over comparable periods. For brand work, show both the raw counts and the formula rather than attaching an unsupported price premium.
