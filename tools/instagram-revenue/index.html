How the Instagram revenue scenario works

Brand-work revenue equals completed deliverables multiplied by the net retained fee. Affiliate revenue equals attributed sales multiplied by the net commission per sale. Live badge and platform bonus revenue are added exactly as entered.

Why the tool does not infer income from followers

Instagram does not publish a universal amount per follower, view, Reel, or post. Deal scope, usage rights, exclusivity, production cost, conversions, program eligibility, geography, and current account offers all vary. A follower multiplier would hide those differences.

What the calculator does not include

It does not estimate reach, determine platform eligibility, create a market rate, assume a percentage of posts are sponsored, calculate tax, or predict an affiliate conversion. Reconcile the result with contracts, affiliate reports, invoices, and retained payouts.
