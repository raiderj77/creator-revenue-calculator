# Creator Revenue Calculator search guidelines

Last reviewed: 2026-09-06

## Principle

Build for the creator decision first. SEO is the technical and editorial foundation for both conventional search and AI-assisted search. There is no separate Google AEO/GEO shortcut, special AI schema, or required `llms.txt` optimization.

Current primary guidance:

- Google AI features and Search: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide
- Helpful, reliable, people-first content: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content
- Spam policies: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies
- Article structured data: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article
- General structured-data rules: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies

## Opportunity selection

- Prefer a verified query/page with meaningful impressions, low CTR, within-reach position, or Generative AI visibility.
- Require a substantial original contribution: a decision framework, transparent formula, reconciliation workflow, downloadable aid, or clearer primary-source synthesis.
- Improve an existing calculator journey before creating a loosely related article.
- Do not turn minor keyword variations into separate pages.

## On-page standard

- Answer the intent immediately, then explain assumptions and limits.
- Use one descriptive title and H1; accurate description; self-canonical; visible published, modified, and review dates.
- Keep metadata, visible content, and JSON-LD exactly aligned.
- Use Article and BreadcrumbList schema when applicable. Do not add schema solely to pursue a rich result.
- Link to primary sources beside factual platform claims and in a visible source ledger.
- Link contextually to the relevant calculator, tracker, and at most a few related maintained pages.
- Use labels such as “synthetic example” whenever numbers illustrate a workflow rather than observed results.

## Prohibited

No fabricated benchmarks or earnings, copied competitor content, doorway pages, hidden text, keyword stuffing, mass-produced thin pages, fake reviews, link schemes, expired-domain tactics, parasite SEO, cloaking, automated engagement, or guaranteed ranking/citation claims.

`llms.txt` is a transparent inventory for interested crawlers. Treat it as documentation, not a ranking lever.
