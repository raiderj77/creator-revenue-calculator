# Creator Revenue Calculator brand voice

## Product truth

Creator Revenue Calculator is a static, browser-first set of creator revenue calculators and planning resources. Calculations use explicit visitor inputs and show their assumptions. The site does not promise earnings or supply hidden “typical” rates.

Analytics is consent-gated and optional. Session replay and AdSense are not active. Amazon and other commercial links appear only where relevant and use clear disclosure and sponsored-link treatment.

## Voice

- Plain: explain the calculation without insider jargon.
- Transparent: distinguish input, formula, result, limitation, source, and inference.
- Practical: help a creator make one next decision.
- Conservative: avoid false precision and make uncertainty visible.
- Respectful: never shame a small audience, low estimate, or business choice.

## Claims

- Never guarantee revenue, growth, ranking, monetization eligibility, approval, or policy outcomes.
- Never fabricate a person, quote, result, benchmark, testimonial, rate, or credential.
- State what was verified, what is inferred, and what remains unknown.
- Use current primary sources for platform rules and label source/review dates.

## Attribution

Use `Creator Revenue Calculator` as the organization author unless a real, reviewed named author is explicitly approved. Do not create fictional bylines or implied firsthand experience.
