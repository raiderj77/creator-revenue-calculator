# Optimize Command

Audit a completed draft or approved page for clarity, evidence, discoverability, and publication integrity.

## Usage

`/optimize [article file or URL]`

## Rules

Read `docs/editorial-publishing.md` first. Optimization is an editorial review, not authorization to modify, publish, merge, deploy, or request indexing.

Do not target keyword density, fixed word counts, a fixed number of headings or links, readability scores, or invented SEO scores. Do not add claims, examples, quotes, schema, dates, or keywords simply to satisfy a checklist.

## Workflow

1. Verify the opportunity record, creator decision, draft status, and associated calculator or next action.
2. Trace every monetary, percentage, platform-policy, eligibility, and time-sensitive claim to the source ledger and current primary source.
3. Check that facts, formulas, user assumptions, labeled synthetic examples, inferences, unknowns, and limitations are distinguishable.
4. Review answer-first clarity, headings, tables, terminology, and CTA for usefulness rather than repetition of a target phrase.
5. Validate title, description, canonical, dates, author, breadcrumbs, visible sources, and Article JSON-LD for exact parity.
6. Verify internal links are contextual and valid; external citations support the exact adjacent claim. Confirm affiliate attributes and disclosures where applicable.
7. Check privacy, consent, accessibility, mobile layout, keyboard behavior, and the generated page against the required build/browser gates.
8. Identify only material fixes. Do not rewrite correct prose to manufacture freshness.

## Output

Return blockers, material improvements, verified strengths, unknowns, and a readiness state: `blocked`, `needs editorial review`, or `ready for owner review`. A readiness state is not publication approval. If edits are requested, keep them in the draft until the separate approval and release gates are completed.
