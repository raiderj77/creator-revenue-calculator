# Research Trending Command

Check whether a creator-revenue topic is changing in a way that merits research or maintenance.

## Usage

`/research-trending [optional topic]`

## Rules

Read `docs/editorial-publishing.md` first. This command is read-only. A short-term impression increase is not proof of durable demand and does not authorize urgent publication or promotion.

Do not use percentage growth without showing both underlying periods and counts. Account for Search Console delay, small denominators, seasonality, query mix, site changes, and missing data. Do not spend on enrichment or promotion without fresh approval.

## Workflow

1. Verify the Search Console property, filters, comparison windows, capture date, clicks, impressions, CTR, and average position.
2. Show absolute values and change for both periods; flag low-volume observations.
3. Check primary platform sources for a real policy, fee, eligibility, terminology, or reporting change.
4. Map the topic to an existing calculator, published article, and creator decision.
5. Distinguish a seasonal pattern, one-off spike, measurement artifact, and sustained change when the evidence permits; otherwise mark it unknown.
6. Recommend monitor, maintain an existing page, conduct deeper research, or reject. New content is a last resort and still requires the editorial opportunity gate.

## Output

Return the compared metrics, primary-source changes, verified facts, inferences, unknowns, and one bounded next check. Do not draft, publish, index, or promote content.
